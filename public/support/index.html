Connecting with your partner

How do we get into the same space?

One of you creates the space and sends the other an invite code that looks like HW5-YEC — three letters, three digits. The other person signs in and enters it. That's the whole setup: there is nothing to search for and no one to add.

"That code has expired"

Invite codes last 48 hours. Ask your partner to open the app and generate a fresh one; the old code stops working and cannot be revived.

"This space already has two people"

A Tweli space holds exactly two people, and the code opens it exactly once. If you're seeing this, the code has already been used — most often because it was entered twice, or on a second device. Ask your partner for a new invite and they'll create a new space to share with you.

"That code wasn't found"

Check the characters. Codes never contain the letters I, L or O, precisely so they can't be confused with 1 and 0 — so if you're reading one of those, it's the digit. Case and hyphens don't matter. If it still fails, the code belongs to a space that no longer exists, and a fresh invite will fix it.

Notifications

I'm not getting my partner's moods or letters

Two things to check, in this order:

  • Permission. iOS Settings → Notifications → Tweli, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If you declined the prompt when you first opened the app, this is almost always the cause.
  • Quiet hours. Between 10pm and 8am in your own timezone, Tweli deliberately delivers without a sound. The notification still arrives — it just doesn't wake you. You'll find it in Notification Centre. You can move or switch off this window under Our space → Quiet hours, and choose which kinds of notification you get at all under Our space → Notifications.

A letter's notification doesn't say what's in it

That's on purpose. A letter's notification says only that one arrived and never previews a word of it, so a glance at your lock screen can't spoil something written to be opened at the right moment.

I set a reminder and my partner heard nothing

Reminders you keep for yourself stay private and send nothing. Set it for them or both of you and it lands on their phone too.

Distance and local time

The distance card is empty

Distance and your partner's local time both come from optional location sharing, and both of you need it on. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Tweli, set to While Using the App.

How precise is it?

City-level only. Tweli asks iOS for approximately kilometre accuracy and never asks for your location in the background — it cannot see your street, and it isn't watching you move. The app works fully without it; you simply won't see the distance or their clock.

Their timezone is missing on a date

If we don't know their timezone yet — because they haven't shared location — Tweli shows only your clock rather than inventing theirs. It'll fill in on its own once they do.

Reinstalling and new phones

I deleted the app. Is everything gone?

No. Sign in with the same Apple ID and your space, your partner, your letters, reminders and countdowns come back down from the server. You do not need a new invite code, and your partner doesn't have to do anything.

The widget is showing old information

The widget refreshes on iOS's schedule rather than instantly. Opening the app updates it. If it's still stale, remove the widget and add it again.

Your account

Deleting your account

In the app: Our space → Your account → Delete my account. This deletes your account and your content from the server for good. It cannot be undone, so the app offers to export a keepsake copy first.

Leaving without deleting

The same screen lets you leave a space while keeping your account, if you want to stop sharing but not erase everything.
