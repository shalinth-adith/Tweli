Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

Tweli is a private space for two people. Everything you write in it is for your partner, and no one else. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and there are no analytics or tracking SDKs in the app. Tweli does not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work. There is no account creation beyond Sign in with Apple, and no data is collected from you in the background.

About location

Location is optional — the app works fully without it, and it is only requested while you are using Tweli. It is never collected in the background. Tweli deliberately requests coarse, city-level accuracy rather than a precise fix, because all it needs is a rough distance. You can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings, and the distance simply stops showing.

What we never do

  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties or data brokers.
  • No public profiles, no feed, no directory — there is nobody to browse.
  • No reading of your letters or moods by us for any purpose other than storing and syncing them.

Who can see your data

Your partner, and nobody else. A space holds exactly two people, and the invite code stops working the moment your partner redeems it. Access is enforced server-side by Firestore security rules, so a person who is not a member of your space cannot read it.

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), which acts as our processor. Their handling is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security policy. Firebase is the only third-party service Tweli uses.

Deleting your data

In the app: Our space → Your account → Delete my account.

This runs a server-side deletion that removes your Firebase account and everything you wrote. If you are the last person in the space, the space and all its contents are deleted outright. If your partner is still there, your records are removed and theirs are left intact.

When you delete, you may choose to leave your sealed letters behind for your partner instead of erasing them. That is opt-in — the default is that everything you wrote goes with you.

Leaving a space is different from deleting your account: your letters are held for 30 days so you can invite your partner back. Deleting your account is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you would rather ask us to delete your data, or want a copy of it, email the address below and we will action it.

Retention

Your content stays in your space for as long as the two of you are using it. Deleting it, deleting your account, or leaving the space removes it straight away. Invite codes expire automatically after 48 hours.

Unused spaces are cleaned up. A space that has fewer than two people in it — because nobody ever joined, or because everyone has left — is deleted automatically, along with anything inside it, after five days without activity. Opening the app, writing anything, or your partner joining all count as activity and reset the clock. A space with both of you in it is never swept.

This is deliberate rather than incidental: an abandoned space is data nobody can reach, and keeping it forever would mean holding your words long after they stopped meaning anything to you.

Children

Tweli is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. The in-app delete covers the deletion right completely; for anything else, email us and we will respond.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who can see it, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change in the app before it takes effect.
